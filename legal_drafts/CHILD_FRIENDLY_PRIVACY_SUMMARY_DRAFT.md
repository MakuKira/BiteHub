# BiteHub Privacy: 

BiteHub helps the PNS community see stall menus, place an order, and check an order’s progress.

## What information can it use?

If you make an account, BiteHub uses your name and email for it. You can also browse and order as a guest without a customer account; guest orders ask for your name and save it with the order. For a school delivery, BiteHub also asks for your school role and, if you are a student, your grade and section so the stall and assigned runner can find you. It also saves the items you order and any message to the stall. A message about an allergy can tell someone about your health, so only write what the stall needs to know.

If you choose delivery, the map point you pick goes with your order. A runner’s phone can share its live location after the runner taps **Start sharing** and allows the phone’s location prompt. Location updates are for that delivery and should stop when it ends.

## Who can see it?

The stall handling your order and the assigned runner may need order details. The customer follows the delivery on the map. Authorized school administrators may access order information for approved school duties. [PNS must confirm and keep this list accurate.]

## Your choices

You can say no to the phone’s location request or pause runner sharing. Delivery tracking will not work while location sharing is off. You can turn off browser alerts in BiteHub or your device settings. To ask a question, correct information, or request help, contact [PNS-approved privacy contact].

## Get help

If something feels confusing or unsafe, stop and ask a trusted adult at school or contact [PNS-approved support/DPO contact]. Read the full [Privacy Notice] for more detail.
